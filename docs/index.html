Research question

How do initial train delays, stochastic station dwell times and timetable recovery buffers affect delay propagation and the probability of significant delays?

This prototype simulates five selected train services across the six common stations between Maradana and Panadura. It uses Monte Carlo simulation and a Markov chain to investigate how initial delays, random operating disturbances and recovery buffers influence train-delay propagation. The model uses illustrative assumptions and is intended for simulation and analysis.

About the model and assumptions

The model uses six stations common to all five trains. A three-state Markov chain (Low, Moderate, High) controls illustrative extra disturbances. Running-time noise is normal with standard deviation 1 minute; extra dwell time is exponential with mean 0.8 minutes; the minimum departure headway is 3 minutes; and the recovery buffer is 2 minutes by default.

The disturbance-variability slider is an additional web-demo sensitivity control; the notebook keeps the running-noise and dwell distributions at their fixed assumed values.

The illustrative transition matrix is [[0.70, 0.25, 0.05], [0.30, 0.50, 0.20], [0.10, 0.30, 0.60]]. These values, regime ranges and stochastic distributions are assumptions, not measured train-level parameters.

Verification: the notebook checks that the matrix is row-stochastic, its analytical stationary distribution is valid, 200,000 empirical transitions agree with the matrix, the deterministic limiting case reproduces the timetable, and the 3-minute headway constraint is respected.

The Sri Lanka Railways Administration Report 2023 and the Auditor General's Annual Report 2024 provide system-wide punctuality context. They do not provide the train-by-train histories needed to estimate route-specific Markov transitions or dwell-time distributions.

Reproducibility: the notebook uses NumPy master seed 20261028. The web demo uses a fixed browser-side seed and displays results within the stated comparison tolerance, while allowing the assumptions to be explored interactively.
